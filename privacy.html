Privacy

Student project demonstration

What this site does with your information

  • Batch IDs and filter choices are handled in your browser and are not sent to or stored by this site.
  • The site has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and does not set cookies of its own.
  • The contact form does not send anything. It only opens your email app, and anything you send from there is handled by your email provider.

Hosting

Wherever the site is published (for example GitHub Pages), the hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

This page describes the demonstration as built. If the project later adds real features, this page must be updated.
